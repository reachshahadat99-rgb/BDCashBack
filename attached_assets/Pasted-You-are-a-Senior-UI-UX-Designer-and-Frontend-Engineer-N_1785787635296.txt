2. Separate Signup Flows — Customer vs Merchant

Do not use one shared signup form with a role dropdown. Two distinct entry points, two distinct forms, two distinct post-signup flows.

Customer Signup (/signup or /signup/customer)

Fields:

Full Name
Email
Phone Number
Password (+ confirm)
Referral Code (optional)
Terms & Privacy checkbox (required)

Flow:

Email/OTP verification (per existing Auth.js OTP-ready setup)

Merchant Signup (/signup/merchant or /sell)

Store Name
Business Type (Individual / Registered Business)
Category(ies) you'll sell in
Phone, Email

Step 2 — Verification

NID or Trade License upload (Cloudinary)
Business Address
Bank/Mobile Wallet details for payout (bKash/Nagad/Rocket/Bank)

Step 3 — Store Setup

Store Logo/Banner upload
Short Store Description
Return Policy acknowledgment (platform's standard return window terms)

Summary of entered info
Terms for Merchants (fee structure disclosure: "No listing fee, success fee only on completed sale") shown explicitly before submit

Post-submit:
